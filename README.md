### 🧠 What I Actually Do
 
I'm an AI Engineer and Automation Architect. My job is to **understand your business deeply** — how value flows, where it bleeds, what your team does manually that they shouldn't — and then engineer systems that handle it autonomously.
 
Most automation fails because it's built around tools, not processes. I map the **business logic first**. Every workflow I build has error handling, fallback paths, alerting, and documentation baked in from day one — because production systems don't get second chances.
 
- 🔭 Core expertise: **n8n enterprise automation**, **multi-agent AI systems (LangGraph / CrewAI)**, **WhatsApp Business API**, **CRM integration & enrichment**, **event-driven workflow meshes**
- 🧩 I specialize in workflows that span **multiple platforms simultaneously** — Salesforce, WhatsApp, Jira, Slack, Shopify, and HubSpot all talking to each other through systems I design
- 🌱 Actively researching: **LLM inference optimization**, **GraphRAG**, **multi-modal AI pipelines**, **autonomous agent memory architectures**
- ⚡ Core belief: **The best automation is invisible — it silently handles everything in the background while your team focuses on work that actually matters**

### 🔁 My Process — Business First, Technology Second
 
```
Step 1 → Process Interview        Map every step of the current human workflow end-to-end
Step 2 → Bottleneck Analysis      Identify exactly where time, money, and leads are being lost
Step 3 → Data & System Mapping    Understand every platform involved and how data moves between them
Step 4 → Architecture Design      Design the full automation mesh — error handling and fallbacks first
Step 5 → Phased Build             Ship iteratively, hardest edge cases prioritized first
Step 6 → Monitoring & Alerting    Every production workflow gets a fallback path and a live alert
Step 7 → Documentation & Handoff  Clients own what I build — full technical and operational docs
```

#### 🤖 Autonomous AI Customer Support Mesh with Knowledge Loop
 
**Industry:** SaaS / Tech Product &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Stack:** `n8n` `LangGraph` `Intercom API` `WhatsApp Business API` `Telegram` `Slack` `Notion API` `Stripe`
 
> **The Problem:** Support volume arriving across email, WhatsApp, and in-app chat — inconsistent resolution quality, long wait times, and no institutional knowledge retained from resolved tickets.
 
**What I Built:**
- **Unified Ingestion Layer:** n8n collects support requests from Intercom, WhatsApp, and email into a single normalized queue regardless of source channel
- **LangGraph triage agent** reads every incoming request and classifies by issue type, urgency, account tier (pulled from Stripe), and prior ticket history
- **GraphRAG-Powered Knowledge Base:** Product docs structured as an entity relationship graph in Notion — agent queries this for high-precision resolution before any human is involved
- **Tiered Escalation Logic:**
  - Tier 1 → AI resolves autonomously, sends solution, marks resolved, logs to knowledge base
  - Tier 2 → AI resolves with low confidence, drafts response, routes to agent for 1-click approve/edit/send
  - Tier 3 → Complex or enterprise account, immediately escalates to senior support with full AI-generated context brief on Slack
- **Self-Improving Knowledge Loop:** Every human-resolved ticket is summarized by LLM and written back to the knowledge base — the system becomes more capable with every edge case it encounters
- Automated CSAT collection 24 hours post-resolution; negative scores trigger senior review with conversation replay
**Outcome:** 65% of tickets resolved autonomously &nbsp;·&nbsp; First-response time reduced from 6 hours to under 3 minutes &nbsp;·&nbsp; Knowledge base grew to 800+ structured articles in 3 months — purely from resolved tickets
